This Seller Policy sets out what ZAYLO expects from sellers. It applies together with our Terms of Service, the Prohibited Items list, the Returns and Refunds policy and the Privacy Policy.

## 1. Becoming a seller
- Apply through Sell on ZAYLO with your shop name, business details, tax identification number and the documents we ask for, such as a business permit and a valid government ID.
- Your shop opens only after ZAYLO approves your application. We may ask for more information or decline an application.
- Keep your shop name, business details, address and contact number current in Seller Center.

## 2. Listing products
- New listings, and edits to existing ones, may be reviewed by ZAYLO before buyers can see them. A listing can be rejected with a reason you can fix and resubmit.
- Each listing must show the real product, its condition, its price in Philippine pesos and its options, such as sizes and colors, with the correct stock for each.
- Sale prices must be genuine. Do not raise a price just to show a bigger discount.
- Products must follow the Prohibited Items list and any registration or labeling rules that apply to them.
- Keep stock accurate and switch off or archive products you cannot ship.

## 3. Handling orders
- Confirm new orders and start processing them promptly. If you cannot fulfill an order, cancel it before it is marked ready to ship; the stock is returned automatically and the buyer is not charged.
- When the parcel is packed, mark the order Ready to ship, print its waybill and attach it to the parcel.
- ZAYLO's logistics partner confirms a pickup date. Have the parcel ready on that date. From pickup onward, the logistics partner and its riders update each delivery stage.
- Pack items securely so they arrive in the condition shown in the listing.

## 4. Commission and earnings
- ZAYLO charges a commission of 10% of the item amount on each completed order. Your earnings and the commission are shown in Seller Center Reports.
- Earnings are counted when the order is delivered. Refunds, chargebacks and adjustments from complaints are deducted from your earnings.
- Payouts are made to the account you register with ZAYLO, on the schedule ZAYLO announces in Seller Center.
- You are responsible for your own taxes, receipts and permits.

## 5. Complaints, returns and ratings
- Respond to complaint cases about your orders with honest information and photos when asked. ZAYLO decides the outcome under the Returns and Refunds policy.
- If ZAYLO finds you at fault, you may be charged for the refund and return shipping.
- Do not offer anything in exchange for a good review, and do not pressure buyers to change a review.

## 6. Communicating with buyers
- Use ZAYLO chat for all order communication and keep it polite.
- Do not ask buyers to pay or complete orders outside ZAYLO.
- Use buyers' names, addresses and phone numbers only to fulfill their orders, and never share or reuse them.

## 7. Performance and enforcement
ZAYLO monitors cancellations, late handovers, complaints and listing quality. If this policy is broken, ZAYLO may remove listings, limit or suspend your shop, hold payouts while a case is reviewed, or close your account. Where we can, we will tell you why and how to respond.

## 8. Contact us
For questions about selling on ZAYLO, use Help and support in Seller Center or email zaylo.shop.dev@gmail.com.
