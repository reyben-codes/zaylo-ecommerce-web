To keep ZAYLO safe and legal, the items below may not be listed or sold. This list is part of our Terms of Service and Seller Policy. It is not complete: anything that is illegal to sell in the Philippines is prohibited even if it is not named here. ZAYLO may remove any listing it considers unsafe or inappropriate.

## 1. Always prohibited
- Illegal drugs, controlled substances and drug paraphernalia.
- Firearms, ammunition, gun parts, explosives, fireworks and items designed as weapons, including replicas that can be mistaken for real firearms.
- Counterfeit, replica or pirated goods, and items that infringe someone else's trademark, copyright or other rights.
- Stolen goods, and items obtained or sold in a way that breaks the law.
- Wildlife, endangered species and products made from them, and live animals.
- Human remains, body parts and bodily fluids.
- Adult and sexually explicit products or material.
- Government-issued documents, IDs, licenses, uniforms and badges, and counterfeit money or stamps.
- Personal information, accounts, passwords, and hacking or spying tools and services.
- Gambling services, lottery tickets and betting items.
- Items that promote hate, violence or discrimination.
- Recalled products and items declared unsafe by the government.
- Hazardous materials such as flammable liquids and gases, pressurized containers, toxic chemicals and pesticides.

## 2. Allowed only when fully compliant
These may be listed only when the seller holds the required permits and the product is properly registered and labeled. ZAYLO may ask for proof at any time.
- Food, food supplements, cosmetics and household products that require registration with the Food and Drug Administration (FDA).
- Medical devices. Prescription medicines are not allowed.
- Alcohol, tobacco and vape products, sold only to buyers aged 18 and above and in line with Philippine law.
- Electrical and electronic products that need a Philippine Standard (PS) or Import Commodity Clearance (ICC) mark.
- Knives and tools, sold for kitchen, work or hobby use only.
- Used, refurbished or second-hand items, clearly described as such with their actual condition.

## 3. Listing rules
- Photos and descriptions must show the actual product you will ship.
- Do not use misleading names, brands or keywords, and do not list one product as another to avoid this policy.
- Do not use ZAYLO to advertise items sold outside the platform.

## 4. What happens if you break this policy
ZAYLO may reject or remove listings, cancel affected orders and refund buyers, hold payouts, and suspend or close the seller's account. Illegal activity may be reported to the authorities.

## 5. Report a listing
If you see a listing that breaks this policy, tell us through Help and support or email zaylo.shop.dev@gmail.com.
